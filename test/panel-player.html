Why Bagging Works: Variance of an Average
  • Average of B models with variance σ² and pairwise correlation ρ
  • Independent errors (ρ = 0): variance drops by a factor of B
  • Correlated trees: the ρσ² term never goes away
  • Random forest: decorrelate trees with random feature subsets
Var = ρσ² + (1 − ρ) σ² / B
