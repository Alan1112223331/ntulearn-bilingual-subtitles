Bagging & Random Forests
  • Bootstrap aggregating: train B models on resampled data
    • Each tree sees a different bootstrap sample
  • Combine by averaging (regression) or majority vote (classification)
  • Random forest: random subset of features at every split
  • Reduces variance without increasing bias much
